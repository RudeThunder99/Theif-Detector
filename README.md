# Theif-Detector
A device to detect unwanted intruders from unknowingly entering any house or premises. Uses a 5v relay module to take reading from a IR sensor, based on its signal detects if a part of the door is removed from the sensor's detection range. If the sensor goes off, the relay immediately turns the buzzer on, ensuring the owner of the house know about the intrusion.

### Why I made this project:
A few years back, my house was intruded by a theif, although nothing was stolen and none were harmed, it still bothered me that anyone could get into my house without me knowing by lockpicking or if the door was unknowingly lest unlocked. This was my inspiration to work on this project.
